# Childhood

I was born on January 10th, 2006, in Bondo, a small town in Siaya County, Kenya, to loving parents — Victor Odolo and Filgona Awuor.

## My Father

My father, Victor, never finished high school. Despite dropping out, he built his early working life around casual mechanical jobs — work he was, at the time, deeply proud of. It was honest, hands-on labor, and he wore it well. Later on, he pursued a different path entirely: he joined a driving school, trained as a professional driver, and stayed in that profession well into my adulthood. Watching him shift from casual mechanic work to a stable driving career taught me early on that reinvention was possible, even without a traditional education behind you.

## My Mother

My mother, Filgona, was — and is — a brilliant woman. She completed her studies up to Form 4, but financial constraints kept her from advancing to a higher institution right away, a setback that could easily have closed doors for good. She didn't let it. She later enrolled in a teachers' training college and became a primary school teacher, turning what could have been an ending into a new beginning.

## My Sisters

I grew up alongside two sisters, Mercy Burnet and Harriet Onyx. All three of us — myself included — were raised to be sharp, capable, and unafraid to stand out. They remain some of the most brilliant and beautiful people I know.
