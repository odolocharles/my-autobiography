# Adolescence

I joined Ugenya Boys' High School on 2nd August 2021, where I excelled and was often head of the class. My interest in STEM grew, and I got deeper into maths, chemistry, biology, physics, and geography. I knew I would one day become a doctor (surgeon) or engineer. I joined numerous science clubs — that's where my passion for innovation started.

My hostile background changed me a lot, and made me into something else — "a living machine," which is ready to do everything. My dad never liked seeing or hearing that I had been seen with a lady, leave alone a classmate, which also made me distance myself from females. I spent most of my time alone, either studying/researching random STEM fields, or watching movies, especially sci-fi and action movies.

Now I was a beast!!! Good at physics, math, and hostile environments. I never showed anybody what I could become; I stayed weak but it grew. I learnt everything I could about technology trends, war, economics, healthcare, and engineering. By the time I reached Form 3, I had branched into fields like medicine, pharmacy, computer science, mechatronic engineering, nuclear engineering. Nobody understood why I wanted nuclear engineering, and it even got attention among teachers, that one of their best learners would want to do weaponry and armoury (as they assumed nuclear means bombs). Anyway, I was still topping, though with little attention among teachers. I kept few friends around who had the same interests as mine, mostly in medicine and engineering.

I did my last exam in high school (KCSE, or Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education) and was confident that I would achieve the A plain and land a medicine course in a good university. Unfortunately, situations and bad luck was by my side — I failed, got a B+. I knew medicine wouldn't work after we received the results, on Jan 10th 2025. I knew it was time to unlock the backup plan (tech), as most of the engineering fields I wanted were not offered in most Kenyan universities, and I lacked the tuition fees too.
